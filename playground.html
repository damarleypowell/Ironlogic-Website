This is a bigram language model — the simplest form of an LLM. It learns which characters tend to follow each other in your text, then samples from those probabilities to generate new text. Real LLMs like GPT use transformer attention across thousands of tokens instead of single characters, but the core idea — learning P(next | context) — is identical. Temperature controls randomness: low = predictable, high = creative/chaotic.
